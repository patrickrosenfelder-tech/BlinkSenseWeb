The screen-work reality

When focus deepens, blinking can fade into the background.

Reading, designing, analyzing, and meeting on screen can make us blink less often than we normally do. It is an easy pattern to miss—especially when the work matters.

How it works

A little more awareness, designed to stay out of your way.

    See simple, private trends over time and find the rhythm that supports your workday.

Questions, answered

Clear about what BlinkSense is—and is not.

We believe thoughtful technology starts with plain language.

Is BlinkSense a medical device?+

No. BlinkSense is an awareness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Does BlinkSense record me?+

BlinkSense is designed not to store or send video away for analysis. It is designed around on-device processing of camera signals.

Will it interrupt my work?+

It is designed to be quiet and adjustable. You choose the level and timing of any awareness cues, and can pause them whenever you need.

Which devices will it support?+

We are beginning with laptop-based desk workflows. Platform availability will be shared with early-access participants as the product develops.
