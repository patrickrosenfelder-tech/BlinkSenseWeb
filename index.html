Why blink rate

Deep focus quietly drops your blink rate — and you won't notice until your eyes do.

Reading, coding, designing, and meetings on screen can cut blink rate well below its resting baseline. BlinkSense keeps a private, real-time readout of that rate so a drop is visible before it becomes discomfort.

How the detection pipeline works

From camera frame to alert,
entirely on-device.

  1. 01

    Capture & landmarks

    The front camera feeds a local vision pipeline — Apple Vision, ML Kit, or ARKit TrueDepth blend shapes — that finds your eyes every frame.

  2. 02

    EAR & calibration

    Left and right Eye Aspect Ratio is computed against its own baseline, calibrated only from plausibly open-eye frames so a closed-eye startup can't poison it.

  3. 03

    BPM & alert zones

    A debounced blink-state machine produces a rolling BPM, compared against healthy / low / critical zones with quiet hours and category-aware thresholds.

Questions, answered

Clear about what BlinkSense is — and is not.

We believe thoughtful technology starts with plain language.

Is BlinkSense a medical device?+

No. BlinkSense is an awareness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Does BlinkSense record me?+

No. All camera analysis happens locally on-device. No camera frames, facial data, or session video ever leave the device or get uploaded — there is no networking path in the tracker.

What is Docked Mode?+

A keep-awake, dimmed near-black overlay for when your phone or Mac sits docked on the desk facing you. It stops the display sleeping or glaring while tracking continues in the foreground — camera access on iOS can't run in the background, so Docked Mode is the accepted workaround for desk-docked counting.

Which devices does it support?+

macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows, Android, and iOS — with a Vision app using the front camera and a Sense app using ARKit TrueDepth on compatible iPhones. Download links for current device-test builds are above; iOS requires TestFlight or Xcode until the Apple Developer account is active.

Will it interrupt my work?+

Alerts are configurable per zone, category-aware, and support quiet hours — you decide when and how BlinkSense speaks up, and can pause it anytime.
